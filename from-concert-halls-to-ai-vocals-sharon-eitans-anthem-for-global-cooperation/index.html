From Concert Halls to AI Vocals: Sharon Eitan's Anthem for Global Cooperation

Sharon Eitan's musical path has taken an unusually wide arc. He began as a composer of art music, writing orchestral and chamber works performed internationally by prominent ensembles, before shifting toward computer science and physics and later writing musicals. More recently, he has turned to releasing pop-rock songs built through an unusual process: recording elaborate demos himself, then producing the finished versions using AI vocals, always working to capture the exact mood and expression present in the original demo rather than letting the technology reshape the emotional intent.
