e.lissa faden Turns Quiet Rage Into Hypnotic Darkness on "Cement"

Anger does not always arrive loudly. Sometimes it lingers beneath the surface, gathering weight until it becomes impossible to ignore. On "Cement", Austin artist e.lissa faden transforms that restrained tension into a dark and hypnotic piece of trip hop, creating a song that feels sensual, unsettling and slightly detached from reality.
