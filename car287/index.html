CAR287 Prepare to Unleash the Power of Prairie Heart and Rock Classics

One does not have to complicate things when writing a love song. Sometimes, the best songs are the ones that use an emotion felt universally, which is that of finding someone to fill a void in our life. This is precisely what CAR287 manage to pull off using the energy of classic rock combined with the sincerity of a heartfelt ballad.

The newest offering from the band features the cheerful nature typical for CAR287, as it combines an outstanding guitar riff with a memorable refrain that will stay long after the song is over. At the heart of it is the theme of connections and relationships, and while the message can easily be sentimental, CAR287 choose to give it some energy, so that the feeling of affection turns into the bright and vivid one.

The live aspect of their songs is essential for CAR287. Coming from Winnipeg, the five-piece rock band has managed to create a name for themselves in the last two years, releasing original tracks and performing energetically. The development of their skills and repertoire was determined not only by recording but by their live performances, helping them understand what is needed in the songs to make them sound great in the concert setting.

The recorded history of CAR287 started with their self-titled EP in 2022. After three years, the band released their first full-length album called "Looking Through the Lens", which contained 13 songs establishing their sound. Drawing from classic rock with a dash of indie music, CAR287 managed to create the music that includes storytelling lyrics inspired by prairie life, which makes the band uniquely Canadian without sounding outdated.

There is an authenticity in the approach used by CAR287. Instead of coming up with grand ideas and declarations, the members of the band are interested in everyday stories, jokes, relationships and the details of real life. All of these elements contribute to the songs' atmosphere along with riffs and rhythm. Even if the subject is something universal as love, there is always an understanding that the members are telling their stories, not following a formula.

It is especially clear in the case of the new track. As soon as the listener hears the guitar riff, he or she receives a clear entry point into the song with the melodic nature, and once the vocals start, the refrain serves as the center of gravity of the composition, providing an element that is catchy and easy to remember after the first try. Thus, it is the combination of accessibility and performance that makes the song stand out.

This feature becomes especially important when it comes to the live performance of the song, and CAR287 have already gained enough experience in this aspect to make their music great. Translating a track from the studio to the stage is one of the biggest challenges in songwriting for any rock band. A track can sound great when listened through headphones, but the real test is when it is being played live in front of the audience, sometimes hearing the song for the first time.


CAR287 have managed to build enough momentum to play their songs in the most popular venues in Winnipeg for the last two years, gradually increasing their audience and perfecting the material, which is now released on the album. The popularity of the band is so high that they regularly sell out shows, creating a type of momentum coming from their interaction with the audience, rather than the moment of fame.

The momentum reached a new height in summer 2026, when CAR287 headlined the Icelandic Festival of Gimli and Rockin The Fields of Minnedosa, which featured Smash Mouth and The Pumps, or Orphan. In addition to the fact that this is quite a change of scenery for the band, it shows how far CAR287 have gone in terms of live performance.

There is a certain connection between CAR287's songs and the location where the band comes from. Winnipeg and prairie surrounding it provide the background for the songs of the band, which often reflect the aspects of ordinary life. CAR287 songs include the directness characteristic of classic Canadian rock and flexibility brought by indie influence.

The production is also important for the maintenance of the balance mentioned above. It is necessary to provide sufficient clarity to perceive the melodic elements, which includes the guitar playing and refrains of the vocal, but preserve the rock aspect of it in terms of the sound's physicality.

CAR287 are going to release songs from the upcoming five-song EP, whose title is yet to be decided. The new EP is going to be the next step on the road from the debut EP to the full-length album and prominent live performances.

Rather than treating each release as an isolated event, CAR287 show that their development involves the improvement of their sound and the discovery of new ways to connect with their audience.

Thus, the latest song of CAR287 perfectly fits into this world. Yes, it is a love song, but what makes it so appealing is the ease with which it conveys the main idea. It uses guitars to add the lift to the track, the rhythm to keep everything moving and the refrains, which are meant to do what the refrains must do, namely, stay in memory. Most importantly, the song has already proven its potential to make an impression on a crowd, where the distinction between performer and listener no longer matters.
