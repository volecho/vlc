Rhythm, Synth, Surrender: Inside The New Citizen Kane's Neon World

Some artists write songs. The New Citizen Kane builds worlds. Working as an independent artist, Kane has spent several releases constructing a multi-sensory pop universe where emotion, identity and instinct collide, treating every project not as a simple collection of tracks but as an immersive narrative stretched across sound, visuals and performance.
