Three Continents, One Bow: William Roblejo's Global Cuban Symphony

Cuban violinist William Roblejo has spent his career proving that a single instrument, in the right hands, can carry an entire cultural conversation. His upcoming fourth album, After The Waves, pushes that idea further than ever, bringing together musicians from Cuba, Iran and Spain in an ambitious international collaboration spanning three continents. Created over more than three years, the album explores the meeting point between Cuban musical tradition and contemporary sounds, filtered through a genuinely diverse range of international influences. After The Waves is set for full release on 20th October.
