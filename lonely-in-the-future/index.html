Feature · Mark Hale · August 16, 2026

The Strokes have showcased their track 'Lonely In The Future' live for the first time

Previously, the band had played 'Lonely In The Future' as part of jam sessions during their recent performances. Notably, they performed a shortened version of the track at South Korea’s Incheon Pentaport Rock Festival in August 2023. However, this latest performance marked the first time the song was presented in its full form within a set.

During the Summer Sonic set, aside from 'Lonely In The Future,' the band also played two other songs from their album 'Reality Awaits'—'Going Shopping' and 'Dine N’Dash.' They interspersed these with fan favorites such as 'Reptilia', 'Last Nite', and 'Someday'.

The Strokes topped the bill at Summer Sonic, delivering an 18-song setlist that included their most recent release. Their performance was a highlight of the festival's opening day at ZOZO Marine Stadium in Chiba.
