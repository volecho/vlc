Indiefferential started as a photocopied zine handed out at basement shows. Ten years later, we're still reporting from the floor — not the press box.

Our Mission

Independent by design, underground by choice.

We exist to document the artists, spaces and scenes operating outside the industry machine — the DIY labels, the warehouse promoters, the designers cutting fabric in their bedroom. No pay-for-play, no algorithm chasing. Just the culture, reported honestly.

Every story runs through the same filter: would this matter to someone standing in the crowd at 3AM? If yes, we write it.
