No Studio Tricks: Filipovich's Unplugged Session From Minsk

There is a particular kind of honesty that only comes through when the studio tricks disappear entirely, and that is precisely the territory Minsk-based indie rock band Filipovich occupy on 'Vzaperti,' a raw, intimate three-track Studio Live session inspired by the iconic MTV Unplugged era. Trading digital production for warm acoustic tones and single-take vulnerability, the release captures the honest, melancholic energy of musicians playing together in one room, with no overdubs and no studio tricks standing between the performance and the listener.
