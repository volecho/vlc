Choosing Yourself: Roses and Rain's Sun-Soaked Anthem of Self-Love

There is a particular kind of freedom that comes from simply walking away, and Roses and Rain capture it beautifully on their dreamy, uplifting new song "Until Further Notice." The track centers on finding freedom, embracing self-love and reconnecting with who you truly are, blending hazy synth textures with an intimate acoustic sensibility that unfolds through warm, layered female harmonies and evocative pop production.
