Atlantic Records Set to Release Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album Featuring Top Artists

Atlantic Records and Rockstar Games have announced the release of 'Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album,' an official soundtrack for the upcoming installment in the Grand Theft Auto series, scheduled for November 19th alongside the game's debut. The album features a genre-defying roster of preeminent artists including Travis Scott, PinkPantheress, Morgan Wallen, and Keith Richards, among others. It contains 34 original tracks with six singles released on September 17th. Each edition includes Atlantic Records exclusives such as liquid-filled vinyl and splatter editions. 

Music has always been integral to Rockstar Games' operations – from their early days at BMG Interactive in the 90s to their recent move into records, including launching CircoLoco Records in 2021 for Ibiza dance music. With sales of over 475 million units across its series and anticipation for 'Grand Theft Auto VI,' the soundtrack promises to capture the electric energy of Grand Theft Auto VI's world. Atlantic Records has positioned itself within Warner Music Group, with Kevin Weaver holding a key role overseeing soundtracks and gaming initiatives.
