Ellijai: Outta Nowhere

Seventeen-year-old indie pop/rock artist Ellijai delivers bright guitars, infectious summer hooks, and raw emotion on "Outta Nowhere."

His music combines cinematic melodies, bold guitar work, and sincere lyrics, sitting comfortably between bedroom pop and indie rock while maintaining a youthful energy.
