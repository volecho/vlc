About

 About Indiefferential

Independent music deserves more than an algorithm. It deserves a lasting story.

Since 2012, Indiefferential has been discovering, reviewing, and championing independent artists from around the world. What began as an online publication has grown into a platform that connects emerging musicians with dedicated fans, industry professionals, and fellow artists.

In 2021, we expanded beyond the screen and launched Indiefferential Magazine in print, because great music deserves a place you can hold in your hands. Unlike a social post that disappears in a feed, a printed issue becomes part of a collection: on coffee tables, in studios, on record store shelves, in libraries. It's a feature that can be revisited for years, not scrolled past in seconds.

Our editorial team is made up of musicians and writers who understand the independent artist's journey firsthand, because many of us have lived it. That's what shapes how we interview, what we ask, and what we choose to cover.

What Featured Artists Get

  • 📬 Promotion to our mailing list of 9,460+ subscribers
  • 🌎 Global retail distribution through Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Books-A-Million, Bookshop.org, Target, Walmart & more (issues appear 6–8 weeks after approval, depending on the retailer)
  • 📻 Music placement on our 24/7 Online Rotation Show for 6 months
  • 🏙️ Feature imagery promoted on billboards and bus stops in cities across the US and Europe, monthly

Why Print Still Matters

  • Longevity — A magazine doesn't disappear in a crowded feed. It stays on shelves, desks, and in personal collections.
  • Credibility — Being featured in a professionally produced publication adds a tangible piece to an artist's press portfolio.
  • Shareability — Print issues pass between fans, venues, studios, and labels, extending the life of every feature.
  • Global Reach — Every issue connects independent artists with readers who are actively looking for new music, not just scrolling past it.

Our reach spans continents — from underground bands in New York to bedroom producers in Tokyo to street artists in Paris. We believe creativity has no borders, and every artist has a story worth telling, whether they're releasing a debut single or headlining international festivals.

Our reputation has earned recognition throughout the music industry. Indiefferential was featured in Oren Sharon's list of the Best Music Blogs to Get Your Music Featured in 2025, recognizing our commitment to thoughtful editorial coverage and artist discovery.

Quality journalism is at the heart of everything we publish. Our editorial team is made up of professional musicians and experienced writers, each holding at least a master's degree, combining academic expertise with real-world industry experience to deliver insightful interviews, reviews, and features.
