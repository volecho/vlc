"This Time We'll Be Winning" by Maddox Jones

Maddox Jones, hailing from Northampton, is gearing up for a highly explosive return with his upcoming single 'This Time We'll Be Winning' releasing on the 29th of May right before the commencement of 2026 FIFA World Cup, an event sure to attract fans from all around the USA, Canada, and Mexico. Amidst this great anticipation for the World Cup, Maddox uses his latest single to convey hope, unity and national pride.

"This Time We'll Be Winning" is simply the quintessential indie song that you can sing along to because of its incredibly soaring chorus. The track reflects the infectious hopefulness and positivity which prevails during football events all around the world. This optimistic song by Maddox Jones is filled with heartfelt lyrics that are complemented by high-quality production value giving it a much needed extra kick above other similar anthems. Combining elements of stadium anthem like "Seven Nation Army" and "Zombie Nation" this track is bound to be a hit.


Matthew Jones: The Voice of Hope

A name synonymous with soulful Christian pop music, Matthew Jones has made a name for himself over in the northeast with his songs that reflect gospel, R&B, CCM, and worship genres. However, lately, the talented musician has been working on a solo album which represents a shift in his musical style as an artist.

Based on his experiences as the parent of two chronically ill children and his mission work in Latin America, Matthew Jones has crafted songs that are catchy and relatable to listeners. His latest full-length album represents his evolution as a singer.


"Bottle": Originality in Nostalgia

In this age of contemporary music, "Bottle" acts as a testimony to the power of originality and nostalgia. This song has been written by Melbourne band led by Jason McKee who has been around for thirty years with the same creative vigor.


Throughout his career, McKee has shown a great talent for creating catchy and timeless songs. His newest release is his debut single "Bottle" that shows his ability to write great music once again despite going through changes throughout the decades. The song reflects the raw energy and rebelliousness which characterized McKee in his teenage years spent in Australia in 1990s battling mental health problems.


Stefanie Michaela: The Voice of Every Moment

An uplifting pop song about not caring about perfection, getting rid of self-doubt and enjoying each and every moment of your life. Stefanie Michaela is a Los Angeles-based singer, songwriter, and mother of five children. She creates honest and uplifting songs that encourage authenticity and inspire people to find beauty in life.

Having returned to making music after years of dedication to bringing up her kids, Stefanie wants to make music that inspires people to feel encouraged, seen, and comfortable being themselves. With her melodic and heartfelt music, she gives a positive summer vibe and makes people appreciate each moment of their life.


Ellijai: Outta Nowhere

Ellijai is a 17-year-old indie pop/rock artist and this song is about big guitars and summer hooks. Ellijai writes songs that are catchy, bright and energetic, yet they retain the raw emotion that is common for his music.

Ellijai creates songs that are raw, cinematic, and direct in emotions. His music combines haunting melody, bold guitars, and sincere lyrics and falls somewhere in between bedroom pop and indie rock.


The Organist Who Hasn't Had His Day Yet

Alexander Ffinch is a master musician whose fingers play the organ just like those of a skilled conductor. Coming from a family famous for music, Alexander started playing organ and piano at the Royal College of Music as a child prodigy. His academic career took him to Oxford University where he received organ scholar, an award that gave him vast experience in teaching.

Having settled down in 1990s Lancaster, Alexander gained his reputation as a resident organist of the prestigious Town Hall where he performed over a hundred recitals and took hundreds of lessons from legendary musicians like Thomas Trotter. Having won first place in St Albans Interpretation Competition in 1999, Alexander reached another level in his career and since then he performed all around the world, UK, Europe, USA and Asia. Currently he is the College Organist at Cheltenham College.


Absolute Redwing Creates a Distortion Symphony

In today's era, music usually belongs to easily classified genres, but one artist creates something beyond any genre. This is the enigmatic personality called ABSOLUTE REDW!NG, who is the author of the "ABSOLUTE REDW!NG" project.

Born in the musical environment, where his lineage dates back to seven years ago, Redwing started his career as a young singer, but soon became interested in exploring the world of music. In 2019, driven by the constant desire for self-expression through experimentation with different musical genres, from the rhythmic and sharp rap music to electronic beats, Redwing created his own musical universe.


Hymns from the Abyss

An esteemed mezzo-soprano presents a fusion of classical tradition, electronics and contemporary compositions on an ambitious new record for LAWO Classics.

The Norwegian mezzo-soprano Hege Høisæter presents "Himmelskygge," an ambitious and impressive new album that showcases the richness and diversity of contemporary Norwegian song. The album is released by LAWO Classics and features an array of performers and composers.


Units of a Musical Storm: Symphony Unleashed of Carl De Villa

When it comes to Australia's entertainment industry, one name is more prominent than others – Carl De Villa. Not only have their performances impressed critics, but Carl De Villa also made a significant place for themselves in Australia's music scene. From indie albums to theatrical productions, Carl continues to wow the audience with their diversity and emotions.

Carl De Villa's recent work becomes a reflection of their talents and skills. An album "War Cry" that came out earlier this year became a representation of the artist's capacity to combine pop, soul, and R&B tracks in a seamless manner. Besides being produced at high-quality standards, the album also features an impressive interaction between the songs and audience by virtue of Carl De Villa's unique voice.



Millo's Anonymous

The song "Anonymous," that premiered on 5th June 2026, is a gloomy and captivating description of the digital era obsession which makes it impossible to distinguish admiration from delusion. In the song, Millo explains how we create fantasies about individuals based on social media posts and dating profiles. Lyrics delivered by Millo in a dark voice reveal a fine balance between being confident and desperate, even being stalker-like and funny.

It all started with Millo experiencing firsthand the duality of fan culture that made her both admired and despised, as she had been receiving death threats from fans for just performing with another artist. The first EP "Method of Madness" that will come out in October becomes a statement work reflecting Millo's many sides as a creator.



Harmonic Surrender: Experimental Electronic Music Journey

"Harmonic Surrender" is another proof of the talent of Hidden Sector who masterfully combines layered harmony, texture, and contained emotional tension. The track starts gradually without following the club structure, thus creating suspended space between movement and stillness. It is perfect for curators of experimental electronic music, cinematic electronica, leftfield techno, ambient-adjacent sound design, and atmospheric electronic composition.

Hidden Sector is the electronic music project of Tony Samuel, a British musician who lives in Dubai. Tony's works are based on electro, Detroit techno, and experimental electronica, focusing on atmosphere, texture, rhythm, and human timing. Being inspired by Kraftwerk and Carl Craig, the music of Hidden Sector becomes electronic but human; structured but unpredictable; it manages to evoke emotions.


Midnight Shadows: A New Stage in Pop Career of Sebastian Rydgren

The release of Midnight Confessions Pt. 1 becomes a major step for Sebastian Rydgren in his growing career. Transitioning from the stage of talent shows to the stage of contemporary pop music, the artist has a range of musical influences and storytelling talents that impress people.

Being born and raised in Järfälla, Stockholm, Sebastian Rydgren has been feeling the power of sound and emotion from the very beginning. His musical education has started when Sebastian was participating in childhood talent shows. Then, he moved to Rytmus and Musikmakarna's Global Songwriting Program.



Bejamalen: Modern Sufi Masterpiece

Amidst the dominance of popular Arabic music, Afaq Art Production has become a symbol of authentic and innovative works. Bejamalen (With Beauty), their latest release, is not only lyrical but also a combination of Eastern Maqams, violin, oud, and orchestra, honoring traditions and modernity.

Behind the release of the song is a rising Egyptian star Ziad Kamal. At the young age of 24 years, he managed to win an honorable spot in the Cairo Opera House by participating and winning the Dr. Ratiba El-Hefny competition. Having an academic degree in mechatronics and Eastern Maqams combined with his musical career, Kamal has become a unique artist.


Emotional Rodeo: Janet Devlin's Riotous Return to Country-Rock

After releasing her anthem titled 'Working For The Man', Irish singer-songwriter Janet Devlin returns with another song that aims to be humorous and emotional as she takes the listeners on a journey of riotous fun with her rock-tinted country track 'Diane'. 'Diane' is another testament to Devlin's talent of blending the classic style of storytelling with contemporary and humorous elements, providing melodies with vivid description of small-town's gossip.

Released on June 30th, this song combines old-style and contemporary elements to bring a unique sound. 'Diane' is Janet Devlin's next addition to her rich discography that includes debut album of Devlin titled 'Running With Scissors'. The song shows the singer's reputation as a unique artist with a strong background in Americana and country music genre.



Under the Canopy: A Musical Tapestry of Hope and Resistance

NYC-based folk-pop artist Rachael Sage has premiered a touching music video to her newly remixed version of 'Nexus' taken from the critically-acclaimed 'Canopy', now available via MPress Records. The stripped-down acoustic remake of the song, titled 'Nexus (Acoustic)', is a lead single of Sage's upcoming acoustic album 'Under My Canopy', due later this year.

The original single was emotional song before her album 'Canopy', all the proceeds going to The Trevor Project, which is a leading mental health organization helping LGBTQ+ youth. The song was written as a dedication to Nex Benedict, a nonbinary student who passed away after being violently beaten by his fellow students at their school.


Harry Kappen's After the Crossing

New album by Harry Kappen is called "After the Crossing". It is the first album the artist has made since moving from the Netherlands to Mexico.

Harry Kappen is always an amazing artist with his combination of rock sounds and deep emotional elements. "After the Crossing" continues to follow the same path with the only difference being that the album represents his transition to a different country and music.


Baby Tell Me: The Summer Anthem

After the huge success of their hit song "The Drop", Tuff Rhythm & dharkfunkh present a new song - "Baby Tell Me". It is definitely worth waiting for this summer anthem!


"Baby Tell Me" is released by the label Tuff Groovez, which focuses on nurturing promising artists. The song immediately sets itself apart from other contemporary songs with its particular feel. Infused with 80s synthwave, the song is built upon a groovy beat.


Infectious Confidence: How TIANA Transcends Tradition with "comme ci comme ça"

The new release of TIANA, an emerging artist, is a brilliant pop anthem titled "comme ci comme ça" that means "like this, like that" in French language. This is an immediate hook aimed to catch listener's attention by its irresistibly catchy melody.

With her unique combination of confident vocals and modern production, TIANA creates an engaging sound. Her song "comme ci comme ça" perfectly mixes traditional pop tempo with modern dance elements.


Iri Antler and her Finish Line

Emerging DIY artist from Hamburg Iri Antler has gained popularity with her latest song "Finish Line". It is a song representing sonic symbol of the modern "daily grind" and the endless pursuit of success and its effects.

"I wrote 'Finish Line' after finding a half-broken coffee machine in my kitchen that sounded like an industrial alarm clock. The piece started with whispers, lullabies and gradually transformed into something inspired by industrial music", says Iri about the process of making the song. The result is a heavy avant-garde dark pop track.


Love Me Out Loud: The UK's Glittering Vocalist Returns

Being recognized as one of the most celebrated vocalists in the UK, Joe McElderry is going to make an impressive return with his shimmering new single, 'Love Me Out Loud'. This exciting disco-pop heartbreak anthem was released on June 19th and explores feelings of frustration and heartache that follow the end of a relationship.

Driven by pulsating beats of disco, twinkling synths and soulful vocals of Joe, the song shows the aftermath of a break-up and complex emotions it can cause. Yet, instead of getting into the depths of heartache, it turns heartache into a positive experience, transforming it into a colorfull and liberating experience. It is the song you want to sing out loud on a glittering dance floor.


The Father's Creed

"Fathers" is a clean and conscious hip-hop record that tells about realities, responsibility and emotional aspects of being a father. The song is lyrical and at the same time modern with the focus on presence, accountability and generational aspect.

Adrian Kniffley presents a realistic and relatable perspective on being a father for both fathers and people thinking about their relationships with their fathers.
