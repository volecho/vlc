01 / The story

Built around artists, not algorithms.

What started as an online publication has grown into a global independent-music platform — with editorial, print, radio, events and artist discovery at its core.

Since 2012, we've followed the artists making noise outside the mainstream. We look for the stories behind the songs, the people behind the projects, and the ideas that deserve a bigger audience.

In 2021, Indiefferential expanded into print. The goal was simple: give independent music something a feed cannot — permanence. A feature can become something you keep, share, revisit and remember.

Our editorial perspective comes from people who understand the creative journey firsthand: musicians, writers and experienced storytellers who care about asking better questions.
