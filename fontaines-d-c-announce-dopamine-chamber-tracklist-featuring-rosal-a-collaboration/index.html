Fontaines D.C. Announce 'Dopamine Chamber' Tracklist Featuring Rosalía Collaboration

Irish alternative rock band Fontaines D.C. has unveiled the track list for their anticipated fifth studio album, ‘Dopamine Chamber’. The album includes a significant collaboration with Spanish pop sensation Rosalía on the song titled ‘Where Is Gone?’ This marks the band’s first ever guest vocalist appearance on a track, even though frontman Grian Chatten had long-hoped to work with Lana Del Rey. 

Other notable tracks in the album include lead single 'Marianne', and fan favourites 'Six Shot Morning' and 'Happy For You'. The full list of songs includes: 1. 'Six Shot Morning' 2. 'Marianne' 3. 'Mimesis' 4. 'Happy For You' 5. 'Demolition' 6. 'Tongue' 7. 'One Man' 8. 'Where Is Gone?' (with ROSALÍA) 9. 'You Miss Me' 10. 'Japan' 11. 'Intimate'.
