Take Me Higher: Blake Rave Turns Resilience Into a Party

There is something refreshingly uncomplicated about "I'm On Fire," the latest release from Blake Rave's Never Broken album. At a time when feel-good music can sometimes drift into cliché, Rave keeps things direct and conversational, delivering an infectious, feel-good pop track built around energetic production, a memorable hook and a distinctive vocal, tailor-made for uplifting, driving, positive-vibes playlists. At its core, it is a song about waking up in the right frame of mind, leaving yesterday behind and embracing the day ahead.
