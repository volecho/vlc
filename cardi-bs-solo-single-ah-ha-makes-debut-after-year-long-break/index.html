Cardi B's Solo Single 'Ah Ha' Makes Debut After Year-Long Break

New York rapper Cardi B has released a new single titled "Ah Ha," her first solo music since the release of her 2025 album, Am I the Drama?. The track was produced by DJ SwanQo and features Cardi's signature humor and sass. Notably, she includes the line "na na na boo boo" in the lyrics.

This single marks a return to form for Cardi B after embarking on an arena tour in support of her second album last year. She also performed two songs from the record, including “Bodega Baddie” and “ErrTime,” on Saturday Night Live earlier this year.

The release comes as part of Cardi's ongoing musical career, which has seen her perform alongside other notable artists such as Billie Eilish at Coachella 2021. With "Ah Ha," Cardi appears to be continuing the momentum from her recent success and is likely aiming to solidify her position in the music industry once again.
