Oasis Teases Upcoming Tour with Slane Castle Concert Release

Oasis have released video teasers hinting at a return to Slane Castle in Ireland ahead of what is expected to be their 2027 tour announcement. The band, known for reunions like Live '25 last year, has been teasing other potential venues and dates including Manchester, Paris, Amsterdam, Boston, Rome, and the Etihad Stadium in Manchester City's home grounds. 

Recent footage of a drone display at the Etihad Stadium featuring the Oasis logo is seen as a strong indication that the band may be planning their next UK tour, which will follow their 2026 Manchester residency. Fans have noted similarities to images taken during an earlier performance by Liam Gallagher and Noel Gallagher in 1995. NME’s review of the new documentary film 'Don’t Look Back In Anger,' released on September 9th, praises its portrayal of Oasis as a band that has not lost their relevance despite years without marketing or promotion.
