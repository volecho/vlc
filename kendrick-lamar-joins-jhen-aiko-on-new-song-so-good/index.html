Kendrick Lamar Joins Jhené Aiko on New Song ‘So Good’

'So Good' is the latest track from Jhené Aiko’s new album, Westside Whimsy. The song features a verse by Kendrick Lamar, marking their continued collaboration after first working together on his mixtape Overly Dedicated in 2010. Their partnership began well before either artist's mainstream success, with Lamar contributing to Aiko's debut EP 'Sail Out' in 2013. Until now, ‘So Good’ marks the duo’s return to a collaborative effort after their last appearance on Playboi Carti’s track ‘Backd00rs’ from Music last year.
