Beyoncé's 'Renaissance' Album Features Diverse Musical Pioneers

The highly anticipated release of Beyoncé’s new album 'Renaissance' is set for next week, and it appears her latest project will feature a wide array of musical talent. The list of collaborators and features on the album includes notable names from dance music's history such as Honey Dijon (‘Cozy’), Skrillex (‘Energy’), and Green Velvet (‘Cozy’). While specific roles for each musician are not specified, it is clear that Beyoncé has enlisted vocalists like Tems (‘Move’) and Grace Jones (‘Move’) to contribute. 

The album will also include tracks that sample renowned artists including Donna Summer, Giorgio Moroder, and Right Said Fred, alluding to a blend of dance, soul, and house sounds. Earlier this year, rumors about Beyoncé's collaboration with Honey Dijon were circulating as part of the growing interest in her upcoming dance-infused music.
