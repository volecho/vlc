Born To Fly: Seema Farswani's Cross-Continental Anthem for Every Girl

Some songs start as a private wish and grow into something much larger. That is the trajectory of "Born To Fly," the new single from Indian American singer-songwriter, composer and producer Seema Farswani, a cinematic soft-rock and global-pop anthem created from Singapore through a cross-border collaboration with musicians from both Singapore and Mumbai. Rooted in courage, empowerment, hope and possibility for girls and women, the track brings together Western rock and South Asian musical textures, weaving soaring guitars, expressive sitar, Hindi vocals and layered contemporary voices into an expansive, cinematic arrangement.
