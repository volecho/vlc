The Why Is in the Rub: Johnny Pears' Midnight Existential Crisis

Some songwriters plan. Johnny Pears waits. Born in South Shields in the UK, Pears channels a deliberately spontaneous style of song composition, treating unpredictability not as a limitation but as his primary creative outlet, the only method he has ever really trusted for making anything half decent.
