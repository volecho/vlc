Stray Kids Make Historic Debut at Rock in Rio with Passionate Performance

What made this debut so historic and loud was evident as fans were left wanting for more. With Stray Kids' performance at Rock in Rio, it seems K-pop has finally found its place at major music events like Rock in Rio. The future looks promising with Stray Kids potentially headlining many more nights of K-pop performances at the festival.
