Come Aboard the Happy Flight: Andrew Shabazz's Love Letter in Sound

Not every love story needs words to be told, and "Happy Flight (Slowed)," the new release from Sharpshooter Soundworks, proves exactly that. Releasing on 25th September 2026, the jazz-infused instrumental track marks a deeply personal milestone, telling the story of a pivotal moment in the life of the artist behind the project, Chicago-based producer and songwriter Andrew Shabazz: the plane journey to meet his wife during their long-distance relationship, and the flight that eventually brought her home to stay.
