Where High Heels Meet Hi-Vis: DreamGood's Fortune-Telling Pop Odyssey

DreamGood have never been interested in staying inside neat genre lines, and their new single "Lifeline" makes that clear from the first listen. Described by the band as a soft-boy introvert's anthem about believing in your own innate intuition, the track has been likened to Adele commingling with Aphex Twin, an electronic, weirdo alt-pop offering aimed squarely at fans of Perfume Genius, Four Tet, Belinda Carlisle and George Michael. It marks the second single from their forthcoming concept album, Paris Novella (Fortune Teller), a project built to explore a kaleidoscope of emotion and ideas, inviting listeners to think, dance and flex in the mirror right alongside it.
