Believe and TuneCore Artists Must Opt In Before Their Music Can Be Used by Suno's AI

Believe, the music distribution platform, has announced that artists must actively consent before their music is made available for use in Suno’s AI training programmes, according to Denis Ladegaillerie, founder and CEO of Believe. The agreement with generative AI platform TuneCore allows its distributed repertoire to be considered by Suno but does not automatically place it into the company's models.
