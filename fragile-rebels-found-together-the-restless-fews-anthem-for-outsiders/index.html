Fragile Rebels, Found Together: The Restless Few's Anthem for Outsiders

The Restless Few trace their roots back more than two decades, to childhood friendships formed in Bologna, Italy, long before the band relocated to London and took on its current shape. This ragtag collective of lifelong friends has been writing music together since primary school, surviving countless demos, abandoned projects and what became an epic search for the perfect lead singer, a search that ultimately led them down an unexpected creative path entirely.
