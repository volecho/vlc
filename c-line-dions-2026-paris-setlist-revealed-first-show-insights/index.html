Céline Dion's 2026 Paris Setlist Revealed: First Show Insights

In a return after six years, Céline Dion opened her 26-date Paris residency at the Plenitude Arena in Nanterre with an audience of over 30,000. This number was part of more than 480,000 total tickets sold for her autumn dates. The concert marked a return to the stage following her public disclosure of being diagnosed with stiff-person syndrome. Dion’s setlist included songs from D'eux, her best-selling French-language album and the first to ever sell over 5 million copies in France. English hits were also featured prominently in the performance. The production aimed to spotlight Dion's voice by minimizing pyrotechnics and other visual effects, keeping the focus on her vocal talent. A number of guests attended the concert including Michelle Obama, Oprah Winfrey, and Thomas Pesquet, a French cosmonaut aboard the International Space Station. The show began 47 minutes late at around 9:10 PM local time but ran for close to two and a half hours until 11:14 PM local time. Dion's performance included emotional renditions of 'On Ne Change Pas' and operatic passages like 'La Wally.' She also debuted her upcoming single, 'Dansons,' with gospel choir accompaniment during the 'Pour Que Tu M'Aimes Encore' segment. The concert concluded with disco-inspired numbers featuring hits such as 'I Feel Love' and a reworked version of 'I'm Alive.'
