Forget the Main Quest: Moose Wrench Find Glory in the Detour

Somewhere between the Nevada desert and the rainy streets of Leeds, a trio calling themselves Moose Wrench decided that the side road was always more interesting than the highway. That philosophy now has an anthem. Their new single, "Mr. Sidequest," lands on 30th September 2026, and it distills the band's entire worldview into three irreverent minutes: the idea that life's richest rewards rarely come from chasing the obvious goal, but from the strange detours taken along the way.
