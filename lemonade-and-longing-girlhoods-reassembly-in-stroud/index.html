Lemonade and Longing: Girlhood's Reassembly in Stroud

There is a version of growing up that has nothing to do with grand declarations and everything to do with picnics, lemonade and the quiet rituals of an ordinary afternoon. That is the world critically acclaimed indie-pop band Girlhood inhabit on their radiant new single "Lemon Aid," the first glimpse into a fresh, more reflective chapter for the group. The track transforms emotional pressure and quiet recalibration into something gentle, hopeful and life-affirming, an invitation to surrender to the moment, dance until the weight lifts, and find fleeting freedom inside the everyday.
