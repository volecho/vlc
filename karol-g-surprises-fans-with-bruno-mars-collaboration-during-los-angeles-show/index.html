Karol G Surprises Fans with Bruno Mars Collaboration During Los Angeles Show

During Karol G’s second sold-out show at Los Angeles’ SoFi Stadium, the Colombian superstar brought out her collaborator Bruno Mars for a live performance of their new collaboration, “Still,” from Karol G’s latest studio album, No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto. The track debuted on Billboard’s Hot 100 in March and features the pair harmonizing about unconditional love despite no longer being together.

The event was notable not only for the performance but also as it marked the filming of “Still”’s music video. Fans were informed through a large video screen that they would be witnessing the song’s official recording right then and there with Karol G and Bruno Mars, who wore matching outfits for added impact.

Karol G opened the power ballad in a sparkling white dress before being joined by Bruno Mars, who sported a flower-embroidered white suit adorned with a cowboy hat. The pair then performed “Still,” the opening track from Bruno Mars’ upcoming album, The Romantic.

The new single has received positive reception, as Karol G revealed she posted a TikTok video discussing it and how it expressed her current emotions naturally without feeling pressured to use another language. "There’s a song on the album that’s entirely in English," she said, emphasizing the importance of organic expression in music creation.
