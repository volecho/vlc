Indiefferential Issue #1 Print Available Now

We are very pleased to announce that the first issue of our flagship magazine is now available. Musicians, athletes, music/film reviews and even video games you need to check out are waiting for you. The theme of this issue is overcoming obstacles.

From musicians that plan their future shows during the war in Ukraine to DAKAR pilots fighting with nature in dangerous deserts. You can also look forward to suggestions of artist releases that have already been released or will be available in the near future. Each artist is tagged along with the people involved in completing this issue with their own features from around the world.

Indiefferential Issue 1 November

 

Print Specifications: US Letter, 8.5×11 in, 22×28 cm, lightweight cover and glossy pages.
