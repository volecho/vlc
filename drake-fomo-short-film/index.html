Drake’s ‘FOMO’ Short Film Returns with Collaborations and Life Advice

Fear of Missing Out, a short film featuring visuals from Drake's HABIBTI and MAID OF HONOUR albums along with new collaborations from various artists including Yeat, PartyNextDoor, Don Toliver, and more, has been restored to YouTube. The film is set for an hour and focuses on themes such as making the most of a summer, forging relationships, and taking opportunities. Drake describes it as 'self funded surrealism' where he writes life advice and shares his personal experiences in hopes that viewers will draw motivation from it. The short film features cameo appearances by various individuals including NBA star Kevin Durant and model Winnie Harlow, alongside scenes from Drake's albums. 

A preview of his interpolation of Ella Lanley’s ‘Choosin’ Texas’ featuring Don Toliver is also part of the film. The 2027 world tour that Drake mentioned during its premiere has been confirmed with him saying it will be his first North American tour since 2023, scheduled to see everyone again across the globe in 2027. FOMO arrives after Drake’s recent album releases including ICEMAN, HABIBTI and MAID OF HONOUR, which helped make him the first artist to simultaneously hold top three slots on the Billboard 200 chart.
