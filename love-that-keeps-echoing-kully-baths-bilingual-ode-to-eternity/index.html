Love That Keeps Echoing: Kully Bath's Bilingual Ode to Eternity

For Kully Bath, love has never been something that simply ends. It transforms, lingers, and returns in unexpected forms, an idea that sits at the center of her new single, "Tu Goonjhda Rehnda Ae" (You Keep Echoing), released on 11th September 2026. The track marks a significant milestone as her first Punjabi-English fusion release, written, performed and conceptualised entirely by Bath herself.
